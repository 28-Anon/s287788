# 13 · THE POWER QUEUE

**Alt 1 — the unreported fact:**

**Alt 2 — direct address:**

> The gas turbines are sold out until twenty twenty nine. That tells you

**Alt 4 — conditional:**
> If the power does not arrive on time, these data centres do not fail

## SCRIPT

They can build the data centre in two years. The power takes ten.

Every AI company is racing to build data centres. Building one takes two to
three years. Connecting it to the electricity grid takes four to ten.

So the buildings get finished long before there is power to run them.

Across America, two thousand six hundred gigawatts of projects are now stuck
in that queue.

In Texas alone, companies have asked for four hundred and seventy four
gigawatts. Ninety percent of that is data centres. That is five times the most
electricity Texas has ever used at once.

This month the governor ordered an audit of all of them.

Build your own power instead, and the gas turbines are sold out until twenty
twenty nine.

Money can fix a funding problem. It cannot fix a queue.

So will the power arrive before the debt is due?

| Texas ~474 GW requested, ~90% data centres | ERCOT interconnection requests | Low. |
| 5× the state's record peak demand | ERCOT all-time peak vs 474 GW | Low. Rounded down from ~5.6×. |
| Governor ordered an audit | Abbott, 3 August 2026 | Low, and dated. Says "this month" — **rewrite to "in August" if posting after 31 August.** |
| Turbines sold out to 2029 | GE Vernova heavy-duty slot allocation | Medium. True of the largest heavy-duty frames, not every turbine on earth. Script says "the gas turbines", which is fair shorthand for camera but is the softest claim in the piece. |

**Nothing here is a prediction.** Every number is a filed request or an
allocated order book. The only forward-looking sentence is the closing
question, which is asked, not asserted.

---

## WHY THIS ONE WORKS

Every AI-bubble video argues about money. This one does not. It says the
constraint is physical, and a physical constraint has a lead time you can look
up. That is a harder, calmer position than "it's a bubble", and it cannot be
falsified by one good quarter.

The closing line is the whole thesis in nine words: **money can fix a funding
problem, it cannot fix a queue.**
