**Notes.** This one changed most, because I had missed the strongest fact available. On the first of March Iranian drones struck Amazon facilities in Bahrain and the UAE — three of the Gulf's 233 data centres. Three is a tiny number and an enormous message, and that tension is the whole video. It is far better than the abstract capital-flows version I wrote first, because it is dated, physical and shootable. Beats 19-20 carry the honest counterweight: Brookfield's twenty-billion partnership with the Qatar Investment Authority went ahead a week after the strikes, and at least one outlet reports no slowdown at all. Leave that in. A prediction that admits the contrary evidence is the one people believe.
