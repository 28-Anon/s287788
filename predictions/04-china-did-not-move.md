**Notes.** The strongest in the set, because it argues against the panic rather than feeding it, and it now rests on named evidence rather than my reasoning. Two facts do the work. Taiwan said in March that Beijing was exploiting the American redeployment, and air incursions did resume after an unusual decline — so pressure is real and the video says so. But the 2026 US Annual Threat Assessment judges that Chinese leaders do not currently plan to execute an invasion in 2027, while Xi has ordered the PLA to be ready by then. Beats 11-13 hold both at once, and 'ready is not the same as going' is the line the whole video turns on. Beat 18 is a genuine falsifier: massing landing craft is observable.
