**Notes.** The strongest thing in this whole project, and every beat is documented. The chain: Saudi Arabia and Pakistan signed a Strategic Mutual Defence Agreement on 17 September 2025; the war opened on 28 February 2026 with US and Israeli strikes on Iran; Iran has since hit Saudi targets including oil facilities and the US Embassy in Riyadh; Saudi Arabia invoked the pact; and on 7 August 2026 the Mecca Joint Defence Agreement brought Turkey in alongside them. Beats 16-18 are the ones to get exactly right and not a word further. This is not a claim that nuclear weapons are coming — it is the observation that a nuclear-armed state is now treaty-committed to a war it did not start. Say that, hold it, and let the viewer do the rest. Overstate it by one sentence and you are a doom account. Understate it and you have the most under-reported story available.
