# 02 — THE STRAIT ALREADY CLOSED

**Thesis:** Hormuz has been shut since February and traffic sits at a fifth of normal. This is not a warning — it is a status report almost nobody is giving.
**Scene:** A petrol station forecourt at night. The camera never leaves it.

**Notes.** This was written as a forecast and it was wrong to write it that way — the Strait has been effectively closed since fighting began in late February and traffic is running at about 20% of the pre-war average. That makes it a stronger video, not a weaker one: it is a receipt, so it cannot be wrong, and almost nobody is stating the plain status. Beats 14-20 are where the forecast lives, and they are conditional, so they hold either way. The Oman talks are the thing to watch — if they break the deadlock the whole picture changes, and beat 18 says so.
