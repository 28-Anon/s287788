# 02 — THE STRAIT ALREADY CLOSED

**Thesis:** Hormuz has been shut since late February and traffic runs at a fifth of normal. This is a status report, not a forecast — and almost nobody is giving it.
**Scene:** A petrol station forecourt at night. The camera never leaves it.
**Runtime:** 69s. 22 beats.

**Notes.** I originally wrote this as a prediction and that was wrong: the strait has been effectively closed since fighting began in late February and traffic is running at about twenty per cent of the pre-war average. As a status report it cannot be wrong, which makes it the safest video in the set. The forecast lives only in beats 15-20 and every one of those is conditional. The Oman talks are the real thing to watch — beat 18 says so, and if they break the deadlock the whole picture changes.
