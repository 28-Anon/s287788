**Notes.** This is his strongest material because it cuts against the panic rather than feeding it, and calm predictions are far harder to sell than scary ones — which is exactly what beats 19-22 are about. The chip argument here is a real published position, sometimes called the broken nest: the value of Taiwan's fabs does not survive being taken. Verified for the script: Taiwan manufactures the large majority of the world's most advanced chips.
