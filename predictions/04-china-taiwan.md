# 04 — CHINA DOES NOT INVADE TAIWAN

**Thesis:** The consensus expects an invasion. The chips make the threat worth more than the taking — and the moment to move was now, and nobody moved.
**Scene:** A phone repair counter. The camera never leaves it.
**Runtime:** 69s. 22 beats.

**Notes.** The strongest one in the set, because it argues against the panic rather than feeding it, and almost nobody in this space does that. Beats 14-16 are the sharpest evidence you have: America is tied up in the Gulf, which is exactly the window an invasion would use, and it has not been used. Beat 17 is the falsifier and it is a real one — ships massing is observable. Ends on a question about media incentives, which is what fills a comment section without starting a fight.
