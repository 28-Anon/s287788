**Notes.** Beat 2 is the load-bearing distinction and the reason this one is defensible: the claim is about the money, not the technology. Keep that separation or the video becomes an AI-is-a-bubble take and inherits every argument that comes with it. The tell in beats 17-21 is genuinely useful — announced data centres are public, quietly cancelled ones are public too, and the gap between the two lists is the story.

## Hook variants

The in-script hook is marked. Test one alternate against it, changing only beats 1–2.

- **A · Dated claim** — HE SAYS THE AI MONEY STOPS IN 2026 · *empty office, monitors glowing*
- **B · Already happening** — THE TECH IS FINE. THE CHEQUE ISN'T. · *a screen going dark*
- **C · The tell** · **IN SCRIPT** — WATCH WHAT GETS QUIETLY CANCELLED · *a phone screen scrolling*
- **D · The receipt** — NOT A BUBBLE CALL. A FUNDING CALL. · *a half-fitted ceiling panel*

**Why it opens the script.** The gap between announced and cancelled is a real, checkable thing almost nobody is looking at, and it promises the viewer something they can go and do.
