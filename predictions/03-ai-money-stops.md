**Notes.** Beat 3-4 is the load-bearing distinction: the claim is about the money, not the technology. Hold that line or it becomes an AI-is-a-bubble take and inherits every argument that comes with it. Beats 14-16 are the most useful thing in the script — this would not look like a crash, it would look like silence, and silence is much harder to notice. The tell in 18-21 is checkable by anyone: announced data centres are public and so are the quietly shelved ones.
