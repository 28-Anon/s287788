# 05 — AI COMPANIONS

**Scene:** A bedroom at night, one phone.  
**Runtime:** ~67s at a natural pace (141 words)

---

The next major product may not be a tool. It may be a relationship.

AI companions are already shipping, and they are not a novelty. However, it is worth remembering what these companies actually sell. They sell attention, and they measure it in minutes.

A feed can be closed. A conversation is harder to close, especially one that notices when you leave.

Therefore, if engagement is the business, the incentive only points in one direction. The small apps have proved it works. The large ones are watching.

If the major platforms stay out of this, I am wrong. But ask yourself why they would stay out. Longer sessions, more days, more revenue. That is the entire model, and loneliness is an enormous market.

This will not arrive as an announcement. It will arrive as a feature in something you already have.
