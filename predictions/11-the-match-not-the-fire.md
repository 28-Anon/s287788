# 11 — THE MATCH, NOT THE FIRE

## Version A — record before the number

Nvidia reports in about an hour, and it could decide which way the market goes.

However, Nvidia is the trigger here, not the cause.

The cause is underneath. Nvidia has put a hundred billion dollars into OpenAI's data centres, and OpenAI is buying Nvidia chips for them. The money moves in a circle, and every pass makes the numbers look bigger.

The largest tech companies have signed over a trillion dollars of data centre leases that have not started yet. Much of that is financed with debt, while borrowing costs sit at nineteen-year highs.

Therefore, if tonight's number disappoints, it does not just hit one company. It questions the whole structure.

The Bank for International Settlements calls this one of the three biggest risks to global stability.

So watch the number. But it is only the match.

## Hook variants

**A** is the one in the script. Swap only the opening sentence.

- **A · Dated claim** · **IN SCRIPT**  
  Nvidia reports in about an hour, and it could decide which way the market goes.
- **B · Contradiction**  
  Nvidia is not the reason the market might crash. It is just the thing that goes first.
- **C · Unreported fact**  
  Five technology companies have signed over a trillion dollars of leases for data centres that do not exist yet.
- **D · Direct address** · **LEAD WITH THIS**  
  If you have a pension, you already own a piece of this bet.

**Why that one.** It makes an abstract market story personal in twelve words, and it is simply true - index-tracking pension funds hold these companies heavily, so most people watching are already exposed whether they follow markets or not. It also earns the right to talk about trillions, because you have established why it is their problem before you reach a single large number.
