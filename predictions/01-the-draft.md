**Notes.** You were right to push on this. Total war deaths and American military deaths are different numbers by a factor of about five hundred, and mixing them is the kind of thing that gets a video torn apart. Beats 4-9 now say exactly whose casualties they are, and the contrast does far more work than a vague 'mounting' ever did: around ten thousand dead, almost none of them American, and that asymmetry is the actual reason the war continues. It also strengthens the draft argument rather than weakening it — the thing that would change is escalation making it expensive for Americans. Beat 20-21 is the kill shot: 'not planned' is not a denial. VERIFY BEFORE SHOOTING: the day count and both casualty figures move — recheck on the morning you record.
