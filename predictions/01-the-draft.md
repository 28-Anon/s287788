# 01 — THE DRAFT COMES BACK

**Scene:** A teenager's bedroom.  
**Runtime:** ~80s at a natural pace (167 words)

---

If Trump remains in office after the midterms, the draft could come back by 2027.

The current conflict with Iran was supposed to be a brief military operation that would cut Iran's capabilities. However, we can see that the war is dragging on. It is past its sixth month now, and American casualties are climbing.

The current president will not allow the country to concede this war willingly. He has called it a little detour. He has called the Strait of Hormuz American territory. That is not the language of a man preparing to withdraw.

Therefore, if he makes it past the midterms, he may escalate rather than concede. And a wider war needs more soldiers than a volunteer army can supply.

America has never switched off its register of young men. The White House says a draft is not the current plan. They have not said it is off the table.

So if you know somebody turning eighteen next year, this is the thing to watch.

---

## Hook variants

Spoken openers. **A** is the one in the script above. Swap only the opening
sentence — and make sure the next sentence still carries the premise.

- **A · Conditional** · **IN SCRIPT**  
  If Trump remains in office after the midterms, the draft could come back by 2027.
- **B · Direct address** · **LEAD WITH THIS**  
  If you are turning eighteen next year, this is the one to pay attention to.
- **C · Unreported fact**  
  America has never switched off its register of young men. It is still running, and every name is still on it.
- **D · Contradiction**  
  Everyone assumes a draft is a last resort. The White House was asked this year, and did not rule it out.

**Why that one.** It turns a geopolitics video into a personal one in fourteen words. The draft is the rare foreign-policy story with a named victim, and naming them in the first line is the whole advantage.
