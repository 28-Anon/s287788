**Notes.** No forecasting needed for the first two thirds — this has already happened and is checkable line by line. Croatia's parliament passed the law in October 2025 and compulsory service for men aged 19 to 29 began in January 2026. Macron launched a ten-month service this summer, aiming at 3,000 in year one and 50,000 by 2035. Germany is sending questionnaires to roughly 680,000 people born in 2008 or later, and it is voluntary. Beats 14-19 are the video: polling finds majorities in France, Germany and Poland support reintroducing service, and that support disappears in the 18-29 bracket. The people voting for conscription are not the people who would be conscripted. Beat 12 is two words and should be delivered completely flat.
