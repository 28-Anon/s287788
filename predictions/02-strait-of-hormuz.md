**Notes.** The tell is the whole video. Tanker war-risk insurance moves weeks before the news does and anyone can look it up, which turns a viewer into a participant — the most screenshotted thing in this genre. Verified for the script: the Strait of Hormuz is roughly 21 miles across at its narrowest and around a fifth of the world's petroleum passes through it. Note the distinction in beat 14 between controlling and closing; his claim is the softer one, and reporting it accurately is what keeps the video right even if he is wrong.

- **C · Already happening** — THE PRICE MOVES BEFORE THE SHIPS DO · *the numbers ticking up*

**Why it opens the script.** It hands the viewer a job in six words. People screenshot tells.
