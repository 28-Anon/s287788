# 08 — ODESSA IS THE REAL FRONT

**Scene:** A kitchen table, one loaf of bread.  
**Runtime:** ~64s at a natural pace (134 words)

---

Odessa is under blockade right now, and that is part of why this costs what it does.

Odessa is Ukraine's only major port. Close it and sea exports stop. Russia has been striking the port complexes for months, and by July the blockade was effective rather than theoretical.

However, notice what has not happened. Russia has not taken the city. Open reporting shows no force assembled for an occupation, at least not this month.

Strangling a port is cheap. Taking a city is not. Therefore, if the blockade holds, Ukraine loses its income without losing the map, which may be the entire point.

If landing forces appear, this reading is wrong. Until then, watch the shipping rather than the front line. The war you are shown is not always the war that decides it.

Spoken openers. **A** is the one in the script above. Swap only the opening

- **C · Unreported fact**  
  Ukraine's only major port has been effectively blockaded since July, and the front line has barely moved.
- **D · The tell**  
  If you want to know how the war in Ukraine ends, stop watching the front line and watch the shipping.

**Why that one.** Six words that reverse the entire expectation, then hand the viewer a puzzle. 'Something cheaper' is a question nobody can leave alone.
