**Notes.** Same shape as the Taiwan script and just as defensible: the pressure is documented, the event is not. Russia has intensified strikes on Odessa's port complexes and export terminals since late 2025, and by July the blockade was effective. But open-source reporting does not show the combined-arms force required for a rapid occupation as of this month, and the script says so at beats 11-12. The loaf is the whole reason this is a video and not a briefing — Odessa is how Ukrainian grain reaches the world, so the blockade lands on a kitchen table rather than a map. Beat 18 is a real falsifier: landing forces are observable.
