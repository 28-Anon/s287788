**Notes.** This is the scoring position in action, and it is the video that separates you from every account repeating forecasts. A widely circulated prediction has Trump running as VP in 2028 with someone else at the top of the ticket. The Twenty-Second Amendment bars a third term, which everyone cites. The Twelfth is the one nobody reads out: no person constitutionally ineligible to the office of President shall be eligible to that of Vice-President. Beats 14-15 matter — lawyers do argue over whether 'ineligible' reaches term limits or only the qualifications clause, and the script says so rather than pretending it is settled. Being the person who read the primary text and reported the disagreement honestly is worth more than being right loudly. Verified: midterms are 3 November 2026, Trump's approval is around 40% against 57% disapproval, and he has called the law prohibiting a third term 'very strong' while keeping the door open.
