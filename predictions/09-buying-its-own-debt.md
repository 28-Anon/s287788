**Notes.** This resolves the contradiction in the source material. One prediction said Treasury rates rise to 6% as buyers refuse; another said they get driven to 0% by financial repression. Both are visible at once, and that tension is the video rather than a problem with it: the market is pushing rates up while the government pushes back. Verified: national debt at $40 trillion, quadrupled since 2008; the 30-year reached 5.33%, a 19-year high, with the 10-year around 4.66-4.74%; Chinese holdings down 14% and Japanese down 6% over the year; and on 19 August the Treasury announced it would double long-dated buybacks from $2bn to $4bn per operation, running 9 September to 4 November. Beats 16-18 are the link back to script 03 — AI build-out borrowing is competing for the same lenders, which is a genuinely under-covered mechanism.
