## TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE BUILDING

**1. There's a live contradiction in the economic slate.** Treasuries to 6%+
*and* Treasuries to 0% are opposite calls. That's either two scenarios stated at
different times or a hedge. **Find the timestamps before using either** — and if
it is a hedge, that's a video in itself, and a fair one.

**2. The "Pax Judaica" framework needs care.** A named long-range framework
about Israel rising as America's order declines, phased 2026–28 "The Quagmire",
2028–35 "The Birth", 2030–40 "operational". Whatever the intent, that structure
maps closely onto a well-known antisemitic conspiracy shape, and coverage
already flags conspiratorial elements in his rhetoric. Building videos on that
strand risks your channel far more than it pays. The Iran, dollar, draft and
Taiwan material is strong enough on its own — **use that and leave this one.**

## SERIES STRUCTURE

Four repeatable formats, so you're not reinventing each time:

1. **The receipt** — "He said X by April. It's August. Here's what happened."
   Checkable, can't be wrong, and nobody else leads with the misses.
2. **The pressure** — "Forget the date. Here's the thing that's actually
   building." Cuba is the model: wrong event, real pressure.
3. **The contrarian** — "Everyone says China invades Taiwan. He says the
   opposite. Here's why." Strongest one he has, because it cuts against the
   panic consensus rather than feeding it.
4. **The tell** — "When you see X, the clock has started." Turns viewers into
   participants; most screenshotted format in the genre.
