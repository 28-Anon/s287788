FIVE PREDICTION SCRIPTS — WHAT'S COMING

22 lines, ~3 seconds each, 69 seconds total.

VOICE: conditional reasoning, spoken by you. 'If X, then Y could happen,
because of Z.' Never 'he says'. The conditional is what keeps you safe —
if the condition doesn't happen, you were never wrong.

Every script contains a line that says what would prove it wrong.
