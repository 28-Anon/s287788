TEN PREDICTION SCRIPTS

Spoken voiceover. Read it as written — full sentences, connectives kept.
Roughly 60 to 80 seconds each at a natural pace.

Break the captions at commas and full stops. The script is the speech,
not the caption cards.

VERIFIED 25 AUG 2026.

01 — THE DRAFT COMES BACK
SCENE: A teenager's bedroom.


If Trump remains in office after the midterms, the draft could come back by 2027.

The current conflict with Iran was supposed to be a brief military operation that would cut Iran's capabilities. However, we can see that the war is dragging on. It is past its sixth month now, and American casualties are climbing.

The current president will not allow the country to concede this war willingly. He has called it a little detour. He has called the Strait of Hormuz American territory. That is not the language of a man preparing to withdraw.

Therefore, if he makes it past the midterms, he may escalate rather than concede. And a wider war needs more soldiers than a volunteer army can supply.

America has never switched off its register of young men. The White House says a draft is not the current plan. They have not said it is off the table.

So if you know somebody turning eighteen next year, this is the thing to watch.

02 — THE STRAIT ALREADY CLOSED
SCENE: A petrol station forecourt at night.


The Strait of Hormuz has been closed since February, and almost nobody is talking about it.

A fifth of the world's oil moves through a gap of water about twenty miles wide. Iran shut it when the fighting started, and traffic through it is now running at roughly a fifth of what it was before the war.

However, we should be clear about what that actually means. Iran did not need to win this war. It only needed to close that one gap, and it has held it closed for six months.

The president now describes the strait as American territory. Talks in Oman are the only way out of this, and they have been deadlocked for months.

Therefore, if the strait stays shut, energy prices do not simply return to where they were. And almost everything you buy has to be shipped.

So when the price of something jumps this winter, this is where it started.

03 — THE AI MONEY GOES HOME
SCENE: An office at night, empty desks.


If the war with Iran keeps going, the money building artificial intelligence may go home.

The Gulf states have pledged more than two trillion dollars towards America's AI build-out. However, in March, Iranian drones struck data centres in Bahrain and the United Arab Emirates. Only three sites out of more than two hundred were hit, which sounds small, but the message was not small at all.

The money paying for AI and the region being bombed are the same place. Three of the four largest Gulf economies are now reviewing where that money goes, including reversing what they promised.

Therefore, if this war continues, the AI build-out could slow. Not because the technology failed, but because the funding went home.

Some deals are still going ahead, so none of this is certain. But announcements are public, and so are quiet cancellations. Watch the second list rather than the first.

04 — CHINA DID NOT MOVE
SCENE: A phone repair counter.


America moved its forces to the Middle East, and China did not invade Taiwan. That is the story nobody is telling.

This was supposed to be the moment. Taiwan said so out loud in March, and Chinese air incursions did pick up again after an unusual quiet period. However, pressure is not the same thing as invasion.

American intelligence assessed this year that Beijing does not currently plan to invade in 2027, even though Xi has ordered the military to be ready by then. Ready is not the same as going.

The chips explain why. Taiwan makes what China cannot yet make, and those factories do not survive being taken. Therefore the threat is worth more to Beijing than the invasion ever would be.

If landing craft start massing, this reading is wrong. Until then, watch what they do rather than what they say.

05 — AI COMPANIONS
SCENE: A bedroom at night, one phone.


The next major product may not be a tool. It may be a relationship.

AI companions are already shipping, and they are not a novelty. However, it is worth remembering what these companies actually sell. They sell attention, and they measure it in minutes.

A feed can be closed. A conversation is harder to close, especially one that notices when you leave.

Therefore, if engagement is the business, the incentive only points in one direction. The small apps have proved it works. The large ones are watching.

If the major platforms stay out of this, I am wrong. But ask yourself why they would stay out. Longer sessions, more days, more revenue. That is the entire model, and loneliness is an enormous market.

This will not arrive as an announcement. It will arrive as a feature in something you already have.

06 — THE NUCLEAR PACT NOBODY MENTIONED
SCENE: A corner shop at night, a small TV on.


Saudi Arabia has invoked a mutual defence pact with a nuclear-armed country, and almost nobody reported it.

Iran has been striking Saudi Arabia since this war began. Oil facilities, and the American embassy in Riyadh. Saudi Arabia signed a defence agreement with Pakistan in September of last year, and Pakistan has nuclear weapons. That agreement has now been invoked.

However, it did not stop there. In August, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkey signed the Mecca Agreement, binding three countries together. One of them is nuclear-armed.

Therefore, if Iran keeps striking Saudi targets, Pakistan is treaty-bound to respond. That does not mean nuclear weapons are coming. It means a nuclear state is now committed to a war it did not start.

If the strikes stop, this quietly goes away. If they do not, it does not. And you will hear about it then, rather than now.

07 — EUROPE IS ALREADY CONSCRIPTING
SCENE: A college corridor, a noticeboard.


Europe has already started conscripting. Not soon. Now.

Croatia made military service compulsory in January, for men aged nineteen to twenty-nine. France launched a new military service this summer. Germany has sent questionnaires to hundreds of thousands of young people asking whether they would be willing to serve. That one is voluntary, for now.

However, here is the part that matters. Polling across Europe finds that majorities in France, Germany and Poland support bringing military service back. But among people aged eighteen to twenty-nine, that support disappears.

Therefore the people voting for conscription are not the people who would be conscripted.

If the war in Ukraine settles, this slows down. If it does not, it spreads. And it is already much further along than most people realise.

If you are under thirty in Europe, this is being decided by people it will never touch.

08 — ODESSA IS THE REAL FRONT
SCENE: A kitchen table, one loaf of bread.


Odessa is under blockade right now, and that is part of why this costs what it does.

Odessa is Ukraine's only major port. Close it and sea exports stop. Russia has been striking the port complexes for months, and by July the blockade was effective rather than theoretical.

However, notice what has not happened. Russia has not taken the city. Open reporting shows no force assembled for an occupation, at least not this month.

Strangling a port is cheap. Taking a city is not. Therefore, if the blockade holds, Ukraine loses its income without losing the map, which may be the entire point.

If landing forces appear, this reading is wrong. Until then, watch the shipping rather than the front line. The war you are shown is not always the war that decides it.

09 — AMERICA IS BUYING ITS OWN DEBT
SCENE: An estate agent's window at night.


America is now buying its own debt, and that is worth understanding.

The national debt has passed forty trillion dollars, roughly four times what it was in 2008. Somebody has to lend that money, and it used to be China and Japan. However, Chinese holdings are down sharply over the past year, and Japanese holdings are falling too.

Fewer buyers means higher rates, and the thirty-year recently hit its highest level in nineteen years.

Therefore, in August, the Treasury doubled the amount of its own bonds it buys back, effectively holding the rate down itself.

There is also a new competitor for lenders. AI companies are borrowing enormous sums to build, and that money comes from the same place.

If foreign demand returns, this eases. If it does not, rates keep climbing. And that is the number on every mortgage.

10 — THE THIRD TERM PLAN DOES NOT WORK
SCENE: A desk, a printed constitution.


The third term plan does not work, and it is not close.

The theory goes like this. Trump runs as vice president in 2028, somebody else runs as president, and then they step aside. It sounds clever.

Everybody cites the twenty-second amendment, which blocks a third term. However, the one that actually settles it is the twelfth. It says that no person constitutionally ineligible to the office of President shall be eligible to that of Vice-President.

Lawyers do argue over exactly how far that reaches, but most read it as covering precisely this. Trump himself has called the law prohibiting a third term very strong, while still keeping the door open.

Therefore, when you hear this prediction, treat it as a message to supporters rather than a legal route.

The thing actually worth watching is November, with his approval sitting around forty per cent.
