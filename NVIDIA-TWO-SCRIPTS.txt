NVIDIA EARNINGS — TWO SCRIPTS, ONE OF WHICH YOU RECORD TONIGHT

Results after the US close, 26 August. Call 5pm ET / 10pm BST.

Both scripts are ~1:02. The middle of each is identical on purpose:
whichever way the number goes, the circular financing and the trillion
dollars of uncommenced leases are unchanged. Only the open and close move.

====================================================================

SCRIPT A — IF THE NUMBER IS GOOD
SCENE: A hallway. Post on the mat, one pension statement.

Nvidia beat expectations tonight, and the market breathed out.

However, this was never really about one company's quarter.

Nvidia has put a hundred billion dollars into OpenAI's data centres, and OpenAI is buying Nvidia chips for them. The money moves in a circle, and every pass makes the numbers look bigger.

The largest tech companies have signed over a trillion dollars of data centre leases that have not started yet, most of it financed with debt, while borrowing costs sit at nineteen-year highs.

A good quarter does not repay any of that. It only delays the question.

Therefore the danger now is relief. Every beat makes the next promise easier to sign, and the commitments get larger.

So watch what gets committed next, rather than what got reported tonight.

  ALTERNATE OPENERS:
    B · Direct address   Nvidia just beat, your pension went up, and nothing underneath it changed.
    C · Unreported fact  The good news tonight was never the risk. The risk is what gets signed tomorrow.
    D · Contradiction    Everybody wanted this number. That is exactly what makes it dangerous.

  LEAD WITH: D

====================================================================

SCRIPT B — IF THE NUMBER IS BAD
SCENE: A hallway. Post on the mat, one pension statement.

Nvidia missed tonight, and the whole market is going to feel it.

However, Nvidia was only ever the trigger. The fire was already built.

Nvidia has put a hundred billion dollars into OpenAI's data centres, and OpenAI is buying Nvidia chips for them. The money moves in a circle, and a circle only works while everybody keeps paying in.

The largest tech companies have signed over a trillion dollars of data centre leases that have not started yet, most of it financed with debt, at nineteen-year-high borrowing costs.

Therefore the question is no longer whether the chips sell. It is whether the lenders stay.

The Bank for International Settlements already called this one of the three biggest risks to global stability.

So watch the credit markets tomorrow, not the share price tonight.

  ALTERNATE OPENERS:
    B · Contradiction    Nvidia is not why the market is falling. It is just what went first.
    C · Direct address   If you have a pension, you found out tonight what it was actually invested in.
    D · Unreported fact  One company missed a forecast, and a trillion dollars of leases just got harder to finance.

  LEAD WITH: B

====================================================================

THE THIRD OUTCOME — READ THIS

Revenue beats but next-quarter guidance disappoints, and the stock falls
anyway. This has happened to Nvidia before, because expectations are priced
so high that meeting them is not enough.

If that lands: use SCRIPT B and change only the opening line to:

  "Nvidia beat its own forecast tonight, and the stock fell anyway."

Everything after it still holds.
