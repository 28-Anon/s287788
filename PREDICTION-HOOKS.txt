PREDICTION HOOKS — FORTY OPENERS

Four per script. 'A' is the one written into the script.

These are spoken sentences, not caption cards. If you swap the hook, the
NEXT sentence has to carry whatever the new opener skipped — an opener that
gives the ending away still needs the premise stated straight after it.

Test by changing only the opening sentence. Same visuals, same voice, same
everything else, or the test tells you nothing. Judge on 3-second retention.

======================================================================

01 — THE DRAFT COMES BACK

  A · Conditional [IN SCRIPT]
     If Trump remains in office after the midterms, the draft could come back by 2027.

  B · Direct address <-- LEAD WITH THIS
     If you are turning eighteen next year, this is the one to pay attention to.

  C · Unreported fact
     America has never switched off its register of young men. It is still running, and every name is still on it.

  D · Contradiction
     Everyone assumes a draft is a last resort. The White House was asked this year, and did not rule it out.

  WHY: It turns a geopolitics video into a personal one in fourteen words. The draft is the rare foreign-policy story with a named victim, and naming them in the first line is the whole advantage.

======================================================================

02 — THE STRAIT ALREADY CLOSED

  A · Unreported fact [IN SCRIPT]
     The Strait of Hormuz has been closed since February, and almost nobody is talking about it.

  B · Direct address
     The reason your shopping costs more this year is a gap of water about twenty miles wide.

  C · Conditional
     If the Strait of Hormuz stays shut, prices do not go back to where they were.

  D · Contradiction <-- LEAD WITH THIS
     Iran did not need to win this war. It only needed to close one gap of water, and it has held it for six months.

  WHY: It is the cleanest statement of the idea in the set, and it reframes a war as a bottleneck. People remember bottlenecks.

03 — THE AI MONEY GOES HOME

  A · Conditional [IN SCRIPT]
     If the war with Iran keeps going, the money building artificial intelligence may go home.

  B · Unreported fact
     In March, Iranian drones hit data centres in the Gulf. Three sites out of more than two hundred, and almost nobody noticed.

  C · Contradiction <-- LEAD WITH THIS
     The threat to artificial intelligence is not that the technology fails. It is who is paying for it.

  D · The tell
     If you want to know whether the AI boom is ending, do not read the announcements. Read the cancellations.

  WHY: It removes the argument you do not want before it starts. Say 'AI bubble' and half the comments are litigating whether AI works; say 'it is who is paying' and they are listening to the actual claim.

04 — CHINA DID NOT MOVE

  A · Contradiction [IN SCRIPT]
     America moved its forces to the Middle East, and China did not invade Taiwan. That is the story nobody is telling.

  B · Conditional
     If China were ever going to take Taiwan, this year was the moment. It has not moved.

  C · The tell
     Everyone is watching the wrong thing in Taiwan. Watch the landing craft, not the rhetoric.

  D · Unreported fact <-- LEAD WITH THIS
     American intelligence quietly assessed this year that Beijing is not planning to invade Taiwan in 2027.

  WHY: 'Quietly assessed' plus a specific finding reads as something you were not supposed to know, and it is completely sourceable. It also makes the calm answer sound like a scoop rather than a shrug.

05 — AI COMPANIONS

  A · Contradiction [IN SCRIPT]
     The next major product may not be a tool. It may be a relationship.

  B · Direct address <-- LEAD WITH THIS
     The next thing they sell you will not feel like a product. It will feel like company.

  C · Conditional
     If attention is what these companies sell, then loneliness is the largest market they have left.

  D · Unreported fact
     AI companions are not coming. They are already here, and the large platforms are watching how they perform.

  WHY: It describes the experience rather than the product. 'It will feel like company' is the line people repeat, and it lands before anyone can decide the topic is beneath them.

06 — THE NUCLEAR PACT NOBODY MENTIONED

  A · Unreported fact [IN SCRIPT] <-- LEAD WITH THIS
     Saudi Arabia has invoked a mutual defence pact with a nuclear-armed country, and almost nobody reported it.

  B · Conditional
     If Iran keeps striking Saudi targets, Pakistan is now treaty-bound to respond. Pakistan has nuclear weapons.

  C · Direct address
     There is a story running underneath this war that you have almost certainly not been told.

  D · Contradiction
     Everyone is watching Iran and America. The agreement that matters most was signed in Mecca this month.

  WHY: Already the strongest opener in the project. Nuclear, a real invoked treaty, and an accurate complaint that nobody covered it. Do not overwrite this one.

07 — EUROPE IS ALREADY CONSCRIPTING

  A · Unreported fact [IN SCRIPT]
     Europe has already started conscripting. Not soon. Now.

  B · Direct address <-- LEAD WITH THIS
     If you are under thirty and living in Europe, this is being decided by people it will never touch.

  C · Contradiction
     Most people in France, Germany and Poland support bringing back military service. Almost nobody under thirty does.

  D · Conditional
     If the war in Ukraine does not settle soon, conscription spreads. It has already started in three countries.

  WHY: The most quietly furious line available, and it is precisely true: the polling majority backing conscription vanishes among the people it would take. Aimed straight at the audience most likely to share it.

08 — ODESSA IS THE REAL FRONT

  A · Direct address [IN SCRIPT]
     Odessa is under blockade right now, and that is part of why this costs what it does.

  B · Contradiction <-- LEAD WITH THIS
     Russia has not taken Odessa, and it may not need to. It has done something cheaper.

  C · Unreported fact
     Ukraine's only major port has been effectively blockaded since July, and the front line has barely moved.

  D · The tell
     If you want to know how the war in Ukraine ends, stop watching the front line and watch the shipping.

  WHY: Six words that reverse the entire expectation, then hand the viewer a puzzle. 'Something cheaper' is a question nobody can leave alone.

09 — AMERICA IS BUYING ITS OWN DEBT

  A · Unreported fact [IN SCRIPT]
     America is now buying its own debt, and that is worth understanding.

  B · Direct address <-- LEAD WITH THIS
     The reason your mortgage costs what it does is being decided by buyers in Beijing and Tokyo.

  C · Contradiction
     The biggest competitor for America's lenders is not another country. It is the companies building artificial intelligence.

  D · Conditional
     If foreign buyers keep pulling back from American debt, rates do not come down. They climb.

  WHY: It converts sovereign debt into a mortgage payment in one sentence. Beijing and Tokyo deciding your rate is a real mechanism and it sounds like a conspiracy, which is the ideal combination.

10 — THE THIRD TERM PLAN DOES NOT WORK

  A · Contradiction [IN SCRIPT]
     The third term plan does not work, and it is not close.

  B · Unreported fact
     Everyone quotes the twenty-second amendment. The one that actually settles this is the twelfth, and almost nobody reads it out.

  C · Direct address
     You have probably seen the prediction that Trump runs as vice president in 2028. Here is why it fails.

  D · Conditional <-- LEAD WITH THIS
     If Trump tried to return as vice president, one sentence written in 1804 would stop him.

  WHY: 'One sentence written in 1804' is the best line in the deck. Specific, verifiable, and it promises a fact rather than an opinion, which is exactly the promise a debunk should make.
