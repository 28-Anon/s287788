THE POWER QUEUE — 1:03

They can build the data centre in two years. The power takes ten.

Every AI company is racing to build data centres. Building one takes two to three years. Connecting it to the electricity grid takes four to ten.

So the buildings get finished long before there is power to run them.

Across America, two thousand six hundred gigawatts of projects are now stuck in that queue.

In Texas alone, companies have asked for four hundred and seventy four gigawatts. Ninety percent of that is data centres. That is five times the most electricity Texas has ever used at once.

This month the governor ordered an audit of all of them.

Build your own power instead, and the gas turbines are sold out until twenty twenty nine.

Money can fix a funding problem. It cannot fix a queue.

So will the power arrive before the debt is due?
