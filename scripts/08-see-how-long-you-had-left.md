**Notes.** Pivot 1 turns the number from a fact into a decision. Pivot 2 turns it
from private into public — and that's the one that lands, because it's already
half-true: insurers and lenders price people on estimated lifespan right now.
The video just makes the number visible.

Beat 11 is three words and the shortest in the script. That's where it turns.
