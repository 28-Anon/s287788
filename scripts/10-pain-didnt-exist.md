**Notes.** Pivot 1 removes the scar — without visible damage there's no record
either. Pivot 2 removes the last backup, your own attention. What's left is a
body with no way to report anything.

**This one is true and you should say so if the comments argue.** Congenital
insensitivity to pain is a real condition, and the people who have it are
seriously injured by exactly this: unnoticed burns, joint destruction, infections
found far too late. The video isn't inventing a consequence — it's describing a
documented one. That makes it the most defensible script in either set.

Play beats 10-13 completely flat. No stings, no music swell. The calm is the horror.
