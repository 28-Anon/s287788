**Notes.** Pivot 1 removes the idea that not saying it means it's gone. Pivot 2
removes the asymmetry — if only one of you remembered, you could still be
forgiven. The true payoff is the uncomfortable one: forgiveness doesn't erase
anything, it relies on the sharpness fading. Take the fading away and you keep
the forgiveness and lose the peace.
