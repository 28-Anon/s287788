**Notes.** Pivot 1 removes the assumption that not-lying and truth-telling are the
same thing. Pivot 2 removes the last exit. The payoff is the true one: almost
nobody actually wants a world without lies, they want the right to withhold —
that right is what privacy *is*, and the script gets there without ever using the
word.
