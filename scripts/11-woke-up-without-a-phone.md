**Notes.** Pivot 1 removes the utility argument — give them cash, a paper ticket
and a watch, and the phone stops being a tool. Pivot 2 removes the last excuse:
fine, you remember one number. You ring it, and it still fails, because nobody
answers a number they don't recognise.

That's the true payoff and it's the non-obvious one. The phone isn't storing your
contacts; it's storing *your identity to the people who already know you*. Without
it you're not cut off from information, you're a stranger to your own friends.

Beat 14 is the whole video. Don't oversell it — flat delivery, no music sting,
just the ringing.

**Casting note.** "She" is whoever the video is for; swap the pronoun freely.
The beat works because someone is waiting, not because of who they are.
